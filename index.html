I've been thinking for a little while about keeping a website or blog of sorts to keep track of all my projects and research and builds and all. Today, I opened up a YouTube channel so that I could keep track of all my completed electronics projects. I'm planning to document Kaputnik and the EHD thruster, along with my oscillator and antenna. However, our school also has a pretty neat makerspace. I've become close enough friends with the makerspace teacher to the point where he doesn't mind if I wander in after school to go carve spoons or solder aimlessly or whatever. So, I'm planning to just grind projects to practice my skills and apply any knowledge I get through a few electrical engineering courses I'm taking. My first project for this school year, which I'm really excited about will be a few logic gates. I learned plenty about them through the BWSI course and Yale program I did, but I've always wanted to build some actual circuits representing them. I figured I'd root around for some transistors in the CS hallway, but after telling my dad about the idea, he was interested as well and offered to help me out... and on these conditions....
