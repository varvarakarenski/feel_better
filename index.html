Have I told you that I almost had a coup organized against me? Freshman year, I started an Astronomy Club for my school, because I wanted a safe space for people to learn and totally geek out about space. We actually get amazing attendance - on busy days we get up to 40 people. We've had a making-plasma-by-microwaving-grapes experiment, and I'm planning on teaching everyone how to make a cloud chamber this year. I give a lot of astrophysics and engineering lectures, and they go well. I try to pick topics complex enough to be interesting, but boil them down to something simple to understand. If I may comment, I think I do a pretty good job ;). Our club advisory is my dearly beloved 9th grade english teacher: Mr. Page. He's an absolute G. He's put up with my shenanigans for years, including late notice reccomendations, essays, and even serving as the mentor for our CubeSat project + Astronomy Club with no knowledge on the subject. He does an icnredible job at supporting all of his students in any way he can. He's listened to a lot of my girl drama as well. (By saying girl drama, I'm also including research project drama, which is by extension woman in STEM drama.) Either way, Astronomy club meets regularly on Thursdays, and it is my ultimate pride and joy. It makes me immensely happy to have people come up to me after a lecture and ask me what books help with getting started on a subject, or where to apply and who to talk to and how to find more resources on astronomy. It's a wonderful feeling to know that I've been able to inspire someone to keep pursuing what makes them happy. It's also great to think that wow... people willingly sign up to hear me talk for 30 minutes straight about my hyperfixation of the week... . Anyway, enough context: Last year we held elections for class officers, as I had been doing social media, lesson planning, and management all by myself. We elected a few people for secretary and vice president, and a guy named Kyle as our historian. Kyle's dad was the track coach, and he was also actually my middle school soccer coach. Amazing coach. What a guy, really - he was a great player and I feel like he really made our team better together. Anyway - Kyle's pitch was that he posessed an astronomical YouTube channel with over 75,000 subscribers (he recently reached 100,000), and he had worked with professional astronomers on a few research projects. Sweet - his videos did well, so that means he'll be good for the club pages, right? In truth, this guy genuinely put in less than zero work for his position. Genuinely useless. Whatever. I kept the club meeting, holding fundraisers and more meetings and outreach events - but as time went on, Kyle began to develop a distaste for my criminally unorthodox mehtods of teaching. By his reasoning, I spoke too much about astrophysics, and not at all about astronomy. MIND YOU: THE ENTIRE PREMISE OF THE CLUB IS THAT LECTURES AND DISCUSSIONS ARE OPEN!!!! ANYONE CAN SIGN UP TO LEAD THEM !!!!! So, I would kindly tell him: Hey Kyle, you can lead a meeting too. Just let me know when you'd like to and on what topic, so I can advertise it. EVERY TIME, he tells me: no, I don't have any material ready. Or: 'No, I don't feel like it.' 'I don't wanna.' Okay - cool, so I can go on teaching right? *Which is not easy - lesson planning is actually really hard :( Time went on, and my friend Taisiya started dating this guy named Anthony. Anthony was in the same discord channel as Kyle - where one snowy robotics club afternoon, Anthony informed me that Kyle was starting to get really radical. He kept textign about how much he hated me and I shouldn't be president and how terrible of a job I was doing as president. I had known near the founding of the club that Kyle was a little jealous of me starting an astronomy club before he did, but I didn't think much of it since I was elected president in the last election. But Kyle kept texting and texting about his hatred towards me - until one day, he began to orchestrate a coup. He attempted to rally people against me to vote for him for president. This really freaked me out, since obviously this kind of gossip never feels good. BUT GET THIS! a. I didn't even hold elections this year - and b. NOR DID HE HAVE MORE SUPPORT THAN ME! People noticed that I had been single-handedly managing the club and gaining funding - so no move was made against my dictatorship due to my sinister, all-powerful ways. muahaha.
